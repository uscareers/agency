Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Do I need previous experience to apply?

Over 80% of our listings — including Amazon Warehouse, Walmart, Aldi, Pepsi, Housekeeping, Cleaning, Food Tester, and Work From Home roles — provide 100% paid on-the-job training.

2. Can I apply for multiple positions at the same time?

Yes! Every job post on US Careers has its own separate application link. You can apply to as many full-time or part-time roles as you like.

3. Are all 2,723 reviews from real candidates?

Yes. We collect post-placement feedback from candidates across all 50 states. We display 130 detailed reviews on-site, including 3-star and 4-star reviews alongside 5-star reviews.

4. How fast will I hear back after clicking Apply?

Urgent hiring categories like Amazon Warehouse, FedEx, Driver Jobs, and Work From Home typically respond within 24 to 48 hours.
